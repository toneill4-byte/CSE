## 📤 Submission Instructions
Once you have completed building the app:

1. **Export your project:** 
   * In MIT App Inventor, go to the top menu and select **"Export selected project (.aia) to my computer"**.
2. **Attach your file:**
   * Go to the submission area, click **Upload**, then click **Browse**.
   * Navigate to your `Downloads` folder and double-click your exported `.aia` project file.
3. **Turn in:**
   * Confirm your `.aia` file is attached and click **Turn In**.
