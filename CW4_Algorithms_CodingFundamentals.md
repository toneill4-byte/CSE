Learning Objective: By the end of the project students should be able to
1.     Understand how to store and retrieve data from a variable. 
2.     Concatenate (join) different data types together i.e. Strings and numeric values.
3.     Develop Algorithmic thinking (Follow a set of steps to produce a desired result).
4.     Develop an understanding of making things move on the screen and locating where items are on the screen.

1.     What components will we need to meet the needs of our user?

2.     When/How often should we test our program? Explain. (Discuss with your elbow partners)
