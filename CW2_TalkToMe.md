# CW 2: Talk to Me App

## Overview
Build the **Talk to Me** app using MIT App Inventor following the instructions below, and submit your completed project file.

---

## 📚 Resources & Tutorials
* 📺 **Video Tutorial:** [Watch Video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vdo8UdkgDD8)
* 📄 **Step-by-Step Instructions:** [Talk to Me Part 1 (PDF)](http://appinventor.mit.edu/explore/sites/all/files/hourofcode/TalkToMePart1.pdf)

---

## 📤 Submission Instructions
* Export your project file (`.aia`) from MIT App Inventor (`Projects` > `Export selected project (.aia) to my computer`).
* Attach and submit the `.aia` file corresponding to your completed **Talk to Me** app.
