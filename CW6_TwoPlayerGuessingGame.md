# Activity 1.1.4: Two-Player Guessing Game

## 🎯 Performance Objective
By the end of the lesson, students will be able to apply conditional statements and variable scope to produce a **Two-Player Guessing Game**.

---

## 📚 Learning Objectives
* Understand when to use local variables vs. global variables.
* Write programs as pseudocode and in natural language.
* Develop an app independently for multiplayer gameplay.
* Recognize sequencing, selection, and iteration as the primary building blocks of algorithms.

---

## 🧠 Prior Knowledge
Building on previous activities that used conditional statements for screen collision events, you will now use conditional logic to provide user feedback (e.g., advising a player to guess higher, lower, or declaring a correct guess).

---

## ⚠️ Key Concepts & Misconceptions
* **Variable Scope:** Pay close attention to variable accessibility. Global variables are accessible anywhere in the program, whereas local variables are confined strictly to their specific block or function.
* **Complex Boolean Logic:** Practice writing pseudocode for real-world scenarios involving `AND` and `OR` operators before building your blocks in App Inventor.
