# Activity 1.1.3: Happy Balance

## 🎯 Performance Objective
By the end of the lesson, students will be able to apply conditional statements and event-driven programming to create an app that allows a user to move an image on a screen by tilting the device and play a distinct sound when the image touches each edge.

---

## 🧠 Prior Knowledge
In previous activities, students used primitive data types (`int`, `double`, `boolean`) and objects (`String`) to build basic algorithmic thinking. This lesson reinforces key concepts—variables, data types, event handlers, and concatenation—while expanding on the accelerometer activity by incorporating conditional statements (selection) to trigger sound effects based on edge collisions.

---

## 🤝 Do Now / Start-up Task
Open **PLTW.org** and **MIT App Inventor**, read the design overview, and discuss the following questions with your partner (5 minutes):

1. What components will we need to meet the needs of our user?
2. When and how often should we test our program? Explain.
